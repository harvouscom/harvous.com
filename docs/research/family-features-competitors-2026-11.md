# Family features: how Harvous compares (research, November 13, 2026)

Question: does any other Bible study or Bible notes app offer family accounts the way Harvous does? Checked for each app:

1. a family or household plan
2. child or teen accounts, and parent consent or COPPA handling
3. a shared family space
4. parents seeing progress without seeing content

Key: **Yes** = confirmed on an official page. **No** = an official document rules it out. **Not found** = official pages say nothing either way (not proof it doesn't exist).

## Verdict

Do not write "the only app that…". Family plans exist elsewhere (Hallow, Dwell, Bible Memory, Lineage Bible). So do parent-managed child profiles (Hallow Kids Profiles, YouVersion secondary profiles, Superbook, Bible Memory Kids), and so does "verifiable parental consent" language (Hallow).

What we found in no other app:

- **A progress-only parent view for teens.** Parents see counts (last in, chapters and books, number of notes), never the notes, and the teen sees the same numbers. Hallow parents see a kid profile by switching into it, which shows everything. A 2020 article said Bible App for Kids sent parents weekly progress updates. We couldn't confirm that feature is current, so treat it as a possible exception.
- **A notebook for writing, built for under-13s,** combined with verified adult consent (KWS) and parent read and export. Hallow's Kids Profiles are prayer and audio. Lineage Bible lets an adult manage minors' study notes in a family feed, but with no verified consent.

Safe wording: describe the features. If we compare, say "one of the few" or "the only one we found", never "the only".
