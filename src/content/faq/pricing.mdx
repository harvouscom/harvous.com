Harvous is **free for personal Bible study** — unlimited notes, scripture pills, highlights, threads, @ mentions, and the rest of the core app. **Harvous Plus** ($7/mo or $49/yr; $35 for the first 99, first year only) includes Review and Shared Spaces hosting — unlimited spaces, up to 50 people each. Joining a space is always free. See [pricing](/pricing/).
