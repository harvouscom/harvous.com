Harvous is **free for personal Bible study** — unlimited notes, scripture pills, highlights, threads, @ mentions, reminders, and the rest of the core app. **Harvous Plus** ($6/mo or $36/yr) includes Review and Shared Spaces hosting — unlimited spaces, up to 12 people each. Joining a space is always free. See [pricing](/pricing/).
