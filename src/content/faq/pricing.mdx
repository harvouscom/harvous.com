Harvous is **free for personal Bible study** — unlimited notes, scripture pills, highlights, threads, @ mentions, reminders, and the rest of the core app. **Harvous Plus** ($6/mo or $36/yr) includes unlimited history, Review, and Shared Spaces hosting — unlimited spaces, up to 12 people each. Joining a space is always free. See [pricing](/pricing/).

History means earlier versions of your notes and your activity trail. Nothing is ever deleted on either plan — free keeps the last 90 days visible, and Plus keeps all of it, going back to any day you worked on a note.
