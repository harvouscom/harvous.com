Harvous is **free for personal Bible study** — unlimited notes, scripture pills, highlights, threads, and the rest of the core app. **Harvous Plus** ($8/mo or $64/yr; Founding $45/yr for the first 99) unlocks Shared Spaces hosting — unlimited spaces, up to 100 people each. Joining a space is always free. See [pricing](/pricing/).
