Harvous works on any device with a modern web browser. For a better mobile experience on your tablet or phone you can "Add to Home Screen" and it becomes more like an app. On iPhone that step is also what lets reminders reach you — Safari tabs can't receive them, so install it first. Reminders are a web feature for now. Mac, iPad, and iPhone apps coming later.
