question: "Do I need an account to try it?"
order: 5.5
---

No. Open Harvous and you can read a chapter, highlight a verse, and write a note straight away — no sign-up, no card.

What you make that way lives on the device you made it on, so signing up is what keeps it: your study saves to your account and syncs across your devices. Anything you wrote before signing up comes with you.
