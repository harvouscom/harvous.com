Yes. [Family](/add-ons/family/) is included with [Harvous Plus](/pricing/): start a family in Settings › My Family and invite up to five more people, who get Harvous Plus at no extra cost. You study together in a shared Family Space, and everyone's own notes stay theirs. Family is on the web for now.

Teens 13 and up have their own accounts. A parent or guardian sees their progress (when they were last in, chapters and books read, how many notes), never the notes themselves. [What parents can and can't see](/blog/what-parents-can-and-cant-see) has the details.

[Child notebooks](/add-ons/family/child-notebooks/) for children under 13 are coming soon, for families in the United States. A parent sets one up with verified consent and stays in charge. See our [children's privacy policy](/privacy/children/).
