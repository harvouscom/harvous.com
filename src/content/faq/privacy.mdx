Just you, unless you want to share it with others. If you decide to share there will be a unique sharable link that is created. Also, you'll see a blue dot telling you it's shared. See everything shared in Settings > Shared.

If you use Connector (part of Harvous Plus), an AI app you connect can read your notes, and can never change them. The only thing it can add is a new note, when you ask for one. Locked notes stay locked. You choose which apps, and you can see and disconnect them in Settings > Connector.
