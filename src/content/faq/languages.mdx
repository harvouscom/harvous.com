The app itself is in English. Scripture pills work across 15 English Bible translations: KJV, NKJV, ESV, NIV, NLT, NET, BSB, NASB 1995, CSB, AMP, and MSG, plus four Catholic editions with all 73 books: NABRE, Douay-Rheims, RSV-2CE, and NRSV-CE. You can switch the translation on any reference, and choose the one new references start in.
