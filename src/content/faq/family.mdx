Yes. Start a family in Settings and your [Harvous Plus](/pricing/) covers up to 5 more people, with Review exercises, unlimited history and Connector for each of them. Your family also gets a Family Space everyone belongs to. Hosting other shared spaces stays with whoever pays.

Everyone keeps their own account and their own notes. Parents can see how their children's study is going (a few counts, when they were last active, and the books they've read) but never what they wrote, and each child sees exactly what their parents see. Families are for people 13 and older, and anyone can leave at any time.
