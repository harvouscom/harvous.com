---
question: "Can I use Harvous for church or small groups?"
order: 9
---

Yes. Use Harvous for personal prep today, and host a Shared Space when your group needs a room that lasts beyond the hour you meet. Joining a space is free; hosting is included with [Harvous Plus](/pricing/).

Churches interested in future organization features (curriculum published to people connected to the church) can [share interest here](/for/churches/#interest).
