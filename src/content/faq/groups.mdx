Yes. Use Harvous for personal prep today, and host a Shared Space when your group needs a room that lasts beyond the hour you meet. Joining a space is free; hosting is included with [Harvous Plus](/pricing/).

Churches can publish to the people connected to them: a teaching plan for each ministry, study material, and Review questions. [Bring your church to Harvous](/for/churches/#interest) — we set it up with you and start a 30-day pilot.
