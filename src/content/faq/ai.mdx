This is a hot topic, and I have strong views on where AI belongs in Bible study. Harvous doesn't use AI to write or change your notes, and nothing in the app generates study content for you.

There is one thing to know about. Connector, part of Harvous Plus, lets an AI app you choose, like Claude or ChatGPT, read your notes so it can answer your questions. It's read-only, it's off until you connect an app, and you can disconnect any app in Settings. What an app reads is handled by that service under its own terms.
