Yes. Turn on **Catholic Bible** under Settings › My Bible and Harvous follows the 73-book canon, including Tobit, Judith, Wisdom, Sirach, Baruch and 1–2 Maccabees. The NABRE, Douay-Rheims, RSV-2CE and NRSV-CE sit at the top of the translation picker, and Home shows Today's Gospel from the Lectionary for Mass. It's free on every plan, and it doesn't change the translation you already read in.
