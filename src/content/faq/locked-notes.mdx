Yes. Set a 4-digit PIN in Settings > Lock PIN, then choose Lock note from any note's menu. Entering your PIN once opens your locked notes for a few minutes, then they lock again.

A locked note is encrypted on your device before it's saved, so Harvous only ever stores text it can't read. That also means we can't get it back if you forget your PIN. Notes in a shared space can't be locked, and your locked notes never show up in one.
