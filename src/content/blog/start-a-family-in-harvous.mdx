Most families don't need another app. They need somewhere to put the question from dinner, so it's still there on Sunday.

That's what My Family is for. One person with [Harvous Plus](/pricing/) starts a family, invites the rest of the household, and you get a Family Space to study in together. Everyone's own notes stay theirs.

Here's how to set it up.

---

## Start the family

Open Harvous on the web at app.harvous.com and go to Settings › My Family. Start a family there. You're its organizer, the person who manages it.

Family is on the web for now. The apps come later.

---

## Invite up to five more

From My Family, invite the people in your household. Each invite is a single-use link that's good for seven days.

<mark>Everyone you add gets Harvous Plus at no extra cost</mark>: unlimited history, Review exercises and Connector. That's you and five more, six people in all.

When you invite someone, you pick what they are in the family:

- **Parent or guardian.** Invites people and sees how a child is doing.
- **Adult.** Shares the space and Plus. Their study stays theirs.
- **Child.** 13 or older, with their own Harvous account and their own private study.

A teen can ask to become an adult in the family later, and anyone can leave any time.

---

## Use the Family Space

The Family Space is threads and notes everyone can add to. The passage you read at dinner. The question from the car. The book you're working through as a house.

Nothing moves into it unless someone puts it there. <mark>Your own notes stay in your own space</mark>, and so does everyone else's.

If you want a start: make one thread for the book you're reading together, and add a note after each time you read it. In a month you'll have a record of what your family noticed.

---

## Turn on Our Bible

Our Bible shows which books the family has been in, together. It never shows chapters, and it never shows who read what. Each person turns it on for themselves.
