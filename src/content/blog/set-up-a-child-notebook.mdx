Child notebooks are <mark>coming soon</mark>. They aren't open yet, and I'd rather tell you how they'll work than promise you a date.

A child notebook is a private place for a child under 13 to write about what they read and wonder. A parent sets it up and stays in charge. It's included with [Harvous Plus](/pricing/), it runs in a web browser, and for now it's only for families in the United States, because the rules for children's information differ from country to country.

---

## Before you start

You'll need Harvous Plus and a family in Settings › My Family. If you don't have one yet, [Start a family in Harvous](/blog/start-a-family-in-harvous) walks through it.

Your child doesn't need an email address or a phone. A first name or nickname is all we ask for.

---

## Three steps, all yours

You'll set a notebook up from Settings › My Family:

1. **Confirm you're an adult.** Kids Web Services, a parent verification service, checks this for us.
2. **See how we protect them.** One short page, before anything is saved.
3. **Give permission and name it.** A first name or nickname.

That permission is your consent, and we keep a record of it with the notebook. <mark>Nothing is saved about your child until you give it.</mark> The details are in our [children's privacy policy](/privacy/children/).

---

## Handing it over

Your child opens their notebook with a one-time link you make, which works for five minutes. Or you hand over your own device in child mode, and when they tap "I'm all done", it comes back to you. A session ends after eight hours.

The first thing they'll do is decorate the cover.

---

## What you control

After setup, you choose the translations and chapters they read. ERV, CEV and NLV are there to start, plus any other translation you add. You can share a chapter in Family Bible and answer what they think about a verse. You can approve your church's kids ministry reading.

You can read and download their notes, end their sessions, and withdraw permission or delete the notebook whenever you want. Withdrawing stops access right away, and the notebook is deleted 30 days later, so you have time to download it.

Each parent with Harvous Plus can set up to six child notebooks. They don't count toward Family's six people.

---

## When they turn 13

Nothing changes on its own. From 13, your child can have their own Harvous account and join your family. [What parents can and can't see](/blog/what-parents-can-and-cant-see) explains how that's different.

When child notebooks open, the [child notebooks page](/add-ons/family/child-notebooks/) will say so.
