Notes are yours. When you want to send one to a small group, a friend, or your
Sunday class, share a single note or a whole thread with a link. No account
needed on the other end.

When the whole group needs to contribute in the same threads, use
[Shared Spaces](/add-ons/shared-spaces/) — hosting is [Harvous Plus](/pricing/);
joining is always free.
