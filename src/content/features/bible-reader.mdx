Expand a scripture dock into the full reader, or start from the canon as a
grid, or type a reference like you would in a note. Either way opens the
chapter full-screen, in 11 translations, cached so it still works without a
connection. Your account remembers where you left off.

The left margin shows every note that covers a verse on the page — tap one to
jump straight back into it. Highlight a phrase while reading and it's the
same highlight you'd make from a note. Start a note straight from the page
when something's worth writing down.
